READ BEFORE DEPOSITING

Risk information

Access to a website is not evidence that a lending market is safe or approved for launch.

Production activation is blocked until IMD bridge and owner risks, executable liquidity depth, holder concentration and the collateral oracle are independently verified. Zero caps are deliberate safety controls.

Collateral and market risk

IMD can lose value abruptly, become illiquid or have transfer restrictions. Liquidators need enough realizable liquidity to sell seized collateral. A quoted oracle price does not guarantee that value can be recovered.

Oracle and stablecoin risk

Stale or divergent prices can stop borrowing and collateral withdrawals. USDC and USDT are issuer-controlled and may depeg, freeze accounts or change implementation. WETH debt exposes borrowers to ETH/USD price increases.

Variable interest

Borrow rates increase with utilization. Accrued interest can bring an otherwise unchanged position into liquidation. Protocol fees are 0%; interest and gas are additional costs.

Administration and availability

Production governance should be a verified multisig with a timelock. A separate guardian can restrict risk. Governance can change risk settings and oracle sources. Read the deployed role addresses and contract documentation before supplying.

Code and deployment status

No deployment is currently verified.

Automated tests are not an independent security audit. This interface verifies configured runtime code hashes; it cannot prove the safety of an upgradeable token implementation or a price source.

Operational responsibility

Monitor your position, allowance, oracle status and liquidation risk. Transactions may be delayed, replaced or reorganized. Recheck final state after every action.
